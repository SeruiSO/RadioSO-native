# Privacy Policy — Radio S O

Last updated: 2026-09-28

Radio S O is a local internet-radio and music player. It does **not** create user accounts.

## What the app uses

- **Internet** to play radio streams, search RadioBrowser, load station icons and (optional) artist photos (Deezer / iTunes Search).
- **IP-based country (no GPS)** to suggest stations “in your region”. Approximate country only; device coordinates are **not** collected.
- **Bluetooth** to auto-start or continue playback in a car / headphones, if you enable BT watch.
- **Local audio files** only if you open the Music tab (you grant storage/audio permission).
- **Notifications** for playback controls.
- **Settings backup** (export/import JSON you choose) stays on your device or the file you share.

## What we do not do

- No GPS / fine or coarse device location permission.
- No advertising ID, no analytics SDK required to use the app.
- Stream URLs and metadata stay on the device unless you export a backup.
